The free key checker

The text you paste into the key checker is analyzed by JavaScript on your own device. It is not uploaded, stored or seen by us. The page's Content Security Policy prevents it from sending your text anywhere.

Analytics (GoatCounter)

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-focused analytics service that does not use cookies. It records:

  • page views (the page path, without query strings), the referring site, and general browser/OS/screen-size/country information that GoatCounter derives from the request;
  • anonymous events: check_run, finding_<type> (e.g. finding_stripe_secret_key: the type of issue only), platform_<name> (which builder was chosen or detected), and scan_request.

We never send pasted code, keys, app URLs or email addresses to analytics. GoatCounter does not store IP addresses in your record; it uses a short-lived hash to count unique visits (see GoatCounter's own privacy policy). If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, the analytics script is not loaded at all.
